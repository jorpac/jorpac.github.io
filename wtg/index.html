WTG – Wannabe The GOAT

From rookie to legend. Swipe to shoot.

You start as a nobody in the lower leagues. One swipe, one shot, one chance to be noticed.

WTG is a football career RPG for Android built around the moment the ball is at your feet. Swipe to shoot, curl it, pick your spot – every goal is earned, and every goal moves your career forward.

Get it on Google Play

What's inside

  • One-minute matches – a fast run of key moments: shots, free kicks, penalties and headers.
  • A whole career – climb from the third division, earn transfers, get called up by your country, and chase league titles, cups, continental nights and world tournaments.
  • Life off the pitch – contracts, agents, sponsors, followers and how you spend your wages.
  • Achievements and leaderboards with Google Play Games.
  • Skill, not wallet – purchases are convenience and support only, never an edge.
