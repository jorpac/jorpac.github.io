Privacy Policy — WTG

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Who we are

WTG ("the app", "the game") is developed and published by jorpac ("we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, contact us at jorpac99@gmail.com.

WTG is an independent, unofficial football career game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any club, league, competition, or person. Some club and competition names may resemble real-world entities; such names are used for identification and descriptive purposes only and remain the property of their respective owners. WTG uses no official crests, logos, kits, or real player names or likenesses.

The short version

WTG has no accounts of its own. Your career save is stored only on your device. We do not collect your name, email, or anything that personally identifies you. To run ads, measure how the game performs, process optional purchases, and offer achievements and leaderboards, the game uses four third-party services — Google AdMob, GameAnalytics, Google Play Billing, and Google Play Games Services — described below.

What data is collected, and by whom

1. Your game progress (stored on your device)

Your career, settings, money, and stats are saved locally on your device. We cannot see this data, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

2. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its ad partners may collect and process device and usage information such as your advertising identifier (Advertising ID), IP address, device type, and ad-interaction events. This may be used for ad measurement and, where permitted, personalized advertising.

  • Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, and limit ad personalization, in your device settings.
  • Purchasing Remove Ads in the app stops ads from being shown.

3. Analytics — GameAnalytics

The app uses GameAnalytics to understand how the game is played (for example: sessions, level progression, where players get stuck, crashes) so we can improve it. This data is anonymous and tied to a random install identifier, not to your identity. It may include device model, operating system, language, approximate country (from IP, which is not stored as-is), and in-game events.

  • GameAnalytics privacy policy: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy
  • We use this data only in aggregate to improve the game.

4. Purchases — Google Play Billing

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Your payment is handled entirely by Google — we never receive or store your card or payment details. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase succeeded so we can grant the item.

  • Google Play's terms and privacy apply: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Achievements and leaderboards — Google Play Games Services

If you use Google Play Games on your device, the app signs in to it automatically so you can earn achievements and appear on leaderboards. Google Play Games Services processes your Play Games player ID and profile (gamer name and avatar), the achievements you unlock, and the scores the game submits (career goals, goals in a season, and career trophies).

  • Your gamer name, achievements, and scores may be visible to other players, depending on your Google Play Games profile settings, which you control in the Google Play Games app.
  • We use this only to show achievements and leaderboards. We do not receive your email address or other Google account details.
  • A small file on your device remembers achievements and scores earned while you were signed out, so they can be sent the next time you are signed in.
  • If you don't use Google Play Games, or you decline the sign-in, the game works normally without these features.
  • Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How the data is used

  • To display and measure advertising (AdMob).
  • To analyze and improve gameplay, stability, and balance (GameAnalytics).
  • To deliver and restore items you purchase (Google Play Billing).
  • To show your achievements and leaderboard positions (Google Play Games Services).

We do not sell your personal data. We do not build advertising profiles ourselves; any ad personalization is performed by Google under its own policies.

Sharing

Data is shared only with the service providers named above (Google/AdMob, GameAnalytics, Google Play, Google Play Games Services), strictly to provide the functions described. We may disclose information if required by law.

Children

WTG is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Because the app contains ads and in-app purchases, we configure ad and analytics partners according to the target-audience and content settings declared in the Google Play Console. If you believe a child has provided information through the ad or analytics partners, contact us at jorpac99@gmail.com.

Your choices and rights

  • Ads: reset/delete your Advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads in device settings; or buy Remove Ads.
  • Analytics: uninstalling the app stops further collection.
  • Achievements and leaderboards: manage your profile visibility, or delete your Play Games data, in the Google Play Games app.
  • Local data: uninstalling the app deletes your saved game from the device.
  • Depending on where you live (e.g., the EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you. Because we hold no directly identifying data ourselves, most requests are handled through the third-party services above; contact us and we will help direct your request.

Data retention and security

We retain no personally identifying data on our own systems. Third-party providers retain data per their own policies. We rely on Google Play and the providers' security; no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

International transfers

The third-party providers may process data on servers outside your country, under the safeguards described in their respective privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game evolves (for example, if we add or remove a service). Material changes will be posted at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
